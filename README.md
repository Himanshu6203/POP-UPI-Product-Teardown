# POP UPI: Rewiring the UPI Rewards Equation

A 13-slide product teardown of **POP**, a UPI payments app built around a rewards currency (POPcoins) and a D2C shopping marketplace. Written as a standalone PM case study — independent analysis, not affiliated with or endorsed by POP.

**[View the deck (PDF)](./POP_UPI_Teardown.pdf)** · **[Download the editable PPTX](./pop_upi_teardown.pptx)**

## TL;DR

POP's business model is genuinely sound — a brand-funded, B2B2C rewards structure that gives users 2% back without POP funding it from its own margin. But the product has a real gap: users earn POPcoins instantly at the moment of payment, then have to consciously re-engage in a separate app flow to actually spend them. That disconnect is the single biggest lever left on the table for retention.

## Methodology

This is an independent teardown built from POP's public positioning, app behavior, and general UPI market context — not from internal data, user research, or POP's own analytics. Any specific numbers used for funnel stages, KPI targets, or competitive comparisons are clearly marked **illustrative** in the deck itself; they exist to demonstrate product-thinking and quantitative reasoning, not to represent POP's real, unpublished metrics.

## Why this project

Built as a product management portfolio piece to demonstrate:
- Reading a business model and explaining *why* it works, not just *what* it is
- Mapping real user flows and identifying where they break
- Turning a diagnosis into scoped, testable product bets
- Tying every recommendation to a specific, measurable KPI
